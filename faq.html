FAQ

Answers to the questions buyers, partners and investors ask us most.

Common questions

What is Riziki Nature Based Solutions?

Riziki Nature Based Solutions is a Kenya-based company that clears invasive mesquite (Prosopis juliflora, known locally as mathenge) with local communities and converts it into biochar. From that one feedstock we offer carbon removal credits, biochar soil amendment and kiln technology. Our proof of concept has run in Baringo County since 2022.

What is mesquite, and why is it a problem in Kenya?

Prosopis juliflora, known locally as mathenge, is an invasive woody weed. The Kenya Forestry Research Institute estimates that it covers about two million hectares of Kenya. It takes over grazing land and farmland and makes it hard for communities to earn a living from their land.

What is biochar?

Biochar is a high-carbon material made by heating biomass with very little oxygen, a process called pyrolysis. Applied to soil, it improves water retention and microbial activity.

How does biochar remove carbon?

The carbon in mesquite wood would normally return to the air when the wood burns or rots. Turning it into biochar locks that carbon into a stable form that stays in the soil for a very long time.

Where do you operate?

We operate in Baringo County, Kenya. We are planning expansion into Kajiado, Turkana and Samburu counties, where mesquite also covers large areas.

Which standard do you follow?

We follow Puro.earth's biochar methodology, a recognised standard for biochar carbon removal.

Do you sell forward credits?

Yes. A buyer can sign a forward offtake, also called a forward purchase, before the credits are issued. The buyer commits today to buy an agreed quantity at agreed terms, and the credits are delivered once they have been verified and issued. It gives the buyer a secure supply of future removals and gives the project confidence to plan its work. We agree volume, price and delivery schedule case by case. Use the contact form to tell us your volume and timing.

How can I buy biochar soil amendment?

Send us a message through the contact form. Tell us what you farm or manage, roughly how much you need and when, and we will reply.

Can I buy or license your kiln?

The Nomad Pyro System is our mobile pyrolysis kiln, built for remote sites where invasive biomass grows. We sell and license it to partners. Send us a message through the contact form to start the conversation.

How do communities benefit?

Local people are employed to clear land and run the kilns, and cleared farms can be farmed again. We agree access to land with communities and landowners in writing, based on free, prior and informed consent. On public land, Kenya's 2024 Carbon Markets Regulations set a 40% community benefit share.
