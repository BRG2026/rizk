Impact

Restoring farmland, creating dignified work and removing carbon, together with the communities of Baringo County.

Our work

Land back in farmers’ hands

Invasive mesquite (Prosopis juliflora, known locally as mathenge) has taken farmland out of use across Baringo County. Riziki works with local families to clear it, turn it into biochar and return the land to farming.

Our field team comes from the communities we work in. Women and men work as harvesters, graders, kiln operators and data officers, and the income they earn stays in the community.
