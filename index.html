Starting in Kenya

Turning woody invasive species into restored land, improved livelihoods and carbon removal

About us

Turning a crisis into a business

  • Land. We clear invasive mesquite (Prosopis juliflora, known locally as mathenge) and bring farmland back into use.
  • Livelihoods. Local people, many of them women, earn steady pay.
  • Carbon. Mesquite becomes biochar, a soil amendment and carbon removal credits.

Our purpose

Mission and vision

Mission

Restoring land from invasive woody species while increasing rural livelihoods.

Vision

Riziki envisions a world without invasive species, where people and planet thrive.

In her words

Daisy’s story

“This company has put my children through school, and I can farm.”

Daisy, Riziki field team

Daisy’s work with Riziki has put her children through school and lets her farm. Riziki clears invasive mesquite from farmland and turns it into a soil-enriching product, so farms we clear can be farmed again. It has also built Daisy’s confidence.

This is what we are here for: restoring land, creating dignified work and building stronger communities, one cleared farm at a time.
