The business

Invest in restoration that lasts

Riziki Nature Based Solutions clears invasive mesquite (Prosopis juliflora, known locally as mathenge) from farmland in Kenya and turns it into biochar. From that one source of biomass we build three lines of business: carbon removal credits, a soil amendment, and licensing of our Nomad Pyro System Kiln.

Our approach is led by the communities we work in and tracked in the field, so your capital drives real change in rural livelihoods and in the land they depend on.

The opportunity

Understanding the opportunity

Mesquite has overrun about two million hectares of Kenya, taking farmland and grazing out of use. Clearing it is hard, slow work, and for years there has been little money to pay for it.

Biochar changes that. Cleared mesquite becomes a stable form of carbon that carbon buyers and farmers both want, so the work of clearing is supported by what it produces and the land can be farmed again.

Riziki is building the team, the kiln technology and the community partnerships to do this across Kenya and, in time, in other countries facing the same problem.

Why Riziki

Why invest in Riziki?

Our model is built on community benefit and measurable results. Your investment supports:

  • Local families. Paid work for the people who clear, harvest and process mesquite, with a share of revenue returning to the community.
  • Farmland. Cleared farms that can be planted again, so land comes back into use and incomes grow.
  • Technology. The Nomad Pyro System Kiln, a mobile pyrolysis kiln that goes to the biomass and that we also license to partners.

Together these give you both measurable climate impact and real rural development.

Proof in the field

Measured impact, tracked in the field

We measure what our work achieves in four ways:

  • GPS mapping of every plot we clear.
  • Kiln sensors that record what each kiln produces.
  • Community interviews with farmers and field workers about what has changed for them.
  • Puro.earth’s biochar methodology as the framework for our carbon accounting.

This gives investors a clear picture of what their capital achieves on the ground.
